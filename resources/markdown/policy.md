This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Plug, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Plug and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Plug processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.

**Team information** — the team(s) you belong to. A team can act as a publisher (building and listing extensions) or as an installing organisation (browsing and installing extensions), or both.

**Marketplace activity** — the extensions your team publishes, their versions and changelogs, and the extensions your team installs or uninstalls.

**Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. What we don't collect

Dot.Plug governs how an extension is allowed to touch a platform — it doesn't govern, model, or store what an extension actually does inside its own granted scope. We don't have visibility into an extension's internal logic, or any per-customer data an installed extension processes once it's running. That data belongs to, and is the responsibility of, the extension's publisher and the installing team.

## 4. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- let your team publish, list, and version extensions, and let other teams browse and install them; and
- keep Dot.Plug secure and prevent abuse.

## 5. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Plug (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 6. Who can see your information

Your account and team information is visible to your own team. Published extension listings (name, description, versions, publisher team) are visible to any team browsing the marketplace, since that's the point of a listing — but your account credentials and private team data are never exposed through a listing.

## 7. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and team data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, this data is removed, except where we're required by law to retain certain records for longer. Published extension listings may be retained in decertified/archived form for marketplace integrity even after a publisher's account is deleted, without identifying the deleted account.

## 8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage, team-scoped access control, and single-use SSO tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

## 10. Cookies

Dot.Plug uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.

## 12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, contact us at privacy@infodot.co.za.
